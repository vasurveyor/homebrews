# How to Make Mead - Dry Traditional Mead FULL PROCESS - 2024 Re-edit

## Recipe

- Batch size: 1 US gallon
- 2.5 lbs (1.133 kg) honey
- 1 cup steeped Assam black tea
- orange peel
- 88 oz water (measured for their vessel)
- Red Star Premier Classique yeast

## Findings

- **creator advice:** A quiet airlock is only a clue; verify completion with hydrometer readings.
  - [21:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bNW-ZsWL1DM&t=1300s): Neutral pressure in that airlock ... might be done. That's not a guarantee; it's just a sign.
- **creator advice:** They state that a lower starting gravity is easier on yeast than overloading the initial must with fermentables.
  - [23:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bNW-ZsWL1DM&t=1390s): Using a lower gravity to start ... tends not to stress the yeast out as much ... overfeeding the yeast ... makes it really hard on your yeast.
- **batch result:** At roughly four weeks and 0.999, they described the dry mead as clean and strongly honey-charactered without sweetness.
  - [32:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bNW-ZsWL1DM&t=1930s): This doesn't [smell sulfury] at all ... smells very clean ... 0.999 ... bone dry ... I taste honey.

# OFFENSIVE Mead? - Step Feed Pyment Mead Recipe

## Recipe

- Batch size: about 1 US gallon
- 96 oz (2.839 L) grape juice
- 1/2 tsp wine tannin
- 1/2 tsp Fermaid O
- 1/2 packet Cote des Blancs yeast
- 3/4 tsp potassium bicarbonate
- 1/2 lb honey to 1.022 (step feed)
- 1/2 lb honey to 1.018 (step feed)
- 1/4 lb honey to 1.010 (step feed)

## Findings

- **creator advice:** For step feeding, add only enough fermentable sugar that the batch would remain acceptable if fermentation stopped at that addition.
  - [21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPur05swq2U&t=1270s): Just in case it doesn't ferment anymore, you can still drink it and it's okay.
  - [24:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPur05swq2U&t=1470s): At what point would I be happy with it if it didn't ferment further ... I probably don't want to add anything more than a quarter pound.
- **batch result:** The staged additions fermented to 1.002 at the recorded checkpoint; the creators said the approach takes longer and still required later stability checks.
  - [28:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPur05swq2U&t=1700s): It did actually ferment to 1.002.
  - [31:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPur05swq2U&t=1870s): Making a step feed takes longer ... if you like things dry you could just let this go another week.

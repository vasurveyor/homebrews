# Honeysuckle Mead -Easy Semi Sweet Mead without Pasteurizing or Stabilizing

## Recipe

- Batch size: about 1 US gallon
- 1/2 oz (15 g) dried honeysuckle
- 2 1/2 lbs wildflower honey
- water
- 1/2 packet Mangrove Jack's M05 yeast
- 1/2 tsp Fermaid O
- 1/2 tsp acid blend
- 1/2 lb honey (step feed)
- 1 1/2 tsp potassium bicarbonate (adjustment)

## Findings

- **batch result:** They deliberately step-fed past the stated yeast tolerance and observed a stable reading of 1.012 without pasteurization.
  - [29:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HxWJQygomxM&t=1750s): It's sitting at 1.012 ... technically a step feed ... surpass the tolerance of that yeast on purpose ... so that you don't have to pasteurize.
  - [31:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HxWJQygomxM&t=1900s): Did it move in a week? If it didn't, that means it's stable ... always want to do two checks a week apart.

# This Mead Looked Disgusting... then THIS Happened: Apple Butter Mead

## Recipe

- Batch size: about 1 US gallon
- 3 x 17 oz jars apple butter
- 2 lbs wildflower honey
- 100 fl oz water
- 1/2 packet Mangrove Jack's M05 mead yeast

## Findings

- **creator advice:** They deliberately limited starting gravity to reduce the risk of a stall rather than relying on the yeast's maximum advertised tolerance.
  - [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MoBq0QayjPY&t=400s): Don't count on it getting that high ... not be super high and cause a stall. We don't want our yeast to get concerned.
- **creator advice:** After backsweetening, they pasteurized by bringing the liquid center to 160 F for one minute.
  - [41:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MoBq0QayjPY&t=2500s): Internal temperature, the middle of the liquid, to 160 F. We're going to hold it there for 1 minute.
- **batch result:** The final tasting reported apple, honey, and spice character; the spices came from the apple butter and oak rather than a separate spice addition.
  - [45:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MoBq0QayjPY&t=2700s): Apples and honey ... we did not add any spices ... spices that was in the apple butter and the spice notes that come from the amber oaking.

# Can We Make Creamsicle Mead? Worth it or no?

## Recipe

- Batch size: about 1 US gallon
- 2.5 lbs (1.13 kg) honey
- 104 oz (2.948 kg) water
- 4 mandarin orange peels
- Lalvin 71-B yeast
- 2.5 grams Fermaid O
- 1/2 vanilla bean (conditioning)
- honey to 1.028 (backsweetening)

## Findings

- **batch result:** The batch fermented from 1.088 to 0.992; the creator calculated 12.96% ABV.
  - [23:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tVJ07b6HdA&t=1380s): We started at 1.088 ... minus our final gravity of 0.992 ... times 135 ... gives us 12.96.
- **creator advice:** Taste before making post-fermentation adjustments.
  - [21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tVJ07b6HdA&t=1270s): The next step of this really does require us to know what it's going to taste like.

# What Homebrew Fermentation Should Look Like -CSB Unpasteurized

## Findings

- **creator advice:** Foam and krausen can be normal early-fermentation behavior; contain possible overflow rather than treating foam alone as failure.
  - [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yv044DK1Ifc&t=135s): They're going to create more foam ... this is not a bad thing ... put your new brew on a cookie sheet that has edges.
- **creator advice:** A still airlock may reflect a poor seal; look for bubbles rising through the liquid and verify with gravity readings.
  - [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yv044DK1Ifc&t=390s): Tiny bubbles ... means you have a fermentation that is active ... if your airlock isn't active, you could just have a bad seal.
- **creator advice:** Replace or clean a clogged airlock and consider a blow-off tube during vigorous fermentation.
  - [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yv044DK1Ifc&t=250s): Keep the one-way valve ... from getting blocked up ... that could cause some problems.

# STABILIZERS in MEAD Making at Home: Mistake?

## Recipe

- Batch size: 1 gallon split into samples
- 2 lbs honey
- water to 1 gallon
- Premier Classique wine yeast
- equal honey additions for backsweetening
- potassium metabisulfite and potassium sorbate (chemical stabilization)

## Findings

- **creator advice:** The creators frame chemical stabilization and pasteurization as tools and do not claim the single comparison settles all recipes.
  - [32:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qupXap5BBOE&t=1950s): This was just the simplest mead ever ... not a definitive test ... it will be different in other recipes.
  - [34:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qupXap5BBOE&t=2050s): I'm not telling you never do this ... These are all tools.
- **batch result:** In this batch, they identified the chemically stabilized samples by a different smell and an acrid, bitter-rind taste; other differences were subtle.
  - [28:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qupXap5BBOE&t=1727s): If I drank one now and another one in 15 minutes, I probably wouldn't know which was which.
  - [29:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qupXap5BBOE&t=1743s): The flavor was different, the smell was different ... the taste has an acrid kind of thing.

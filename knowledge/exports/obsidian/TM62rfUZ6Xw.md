# Allulose Stops Yeast? -Sweet, Bubbly, 14% ABV Mead

## Recipe

- Batch size: 1 US gallon
- 3 lb (1.36 kg) raw honey
- 96 oz (2.84 L) warm water
- 1/2 packet Mangrove Jack's M02 cider yeast
- 1/2 tsp Fermaid O
- allulose to taste (post-fermentation sweetening)
- 42 g (1.5 oz) sugar (bottle priming)
- a few grains Lalvin EC-1118 (insurance yeast)

## Findings

- **creator advice:** They used non-fermentable allulose to retain sweetness while reserving measured sugar for natural carbonation.
  - [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TM62rfUZ6Xw&t=715s): We want this to be sparkling ... didn't want to have to pasteurize ... we're going to be using allulose.
  - [15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TM62rfUZ6Xw&t=900s): We now need 42 g of sugar ... a stable brew ... that's also sweet ... we want this to be bubbly.
- **batch result:** The finished batch was 1.026 and about 14% ABV, but carbonation was light rather than fully sparkling.
  - [24:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TM62rfUZ6Xw&t=1440s): This is 1.026 final gravity and 14%. Now this we sweetened with allulose.
  - [24:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TM62rfUZ6Xw&t=1460s): It's carbonated. Not much. It's like petulant. Very sweet.

# Fermentation Fundamentals - More Yeast Is NOT the Answer!

## Findings

- **creator advice:** Use unchanged gravity readings one week apart, rather than bubbles alone, to judge stability.
  - [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=euOXXbi0f9A&t=300s): Wait a week, take another reading. If those two readings don't change, it's done ... stable.
- **creator advice:** For the discussed sluggish step-fed fermentation, they advised waiting before adding more yeast and considering removal of oak until conditioning.
  - [18:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=euOXXbi0f9A&t=1120s): Sluggish fermentation ... I told him to sit back and wait ... I probably would not have put more yeast in yet.
  - [19:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=euOXXbi0f9A&t=1180s): Let it go for a while ... if not, I would probably get those wood chips out ... put them in secondary.
